THERMODYNAMIC PHYSICS ENGINE - FORMULA DOCUMENTATION
Author: Harnoor Kant

This document outlines the exact mathematical formulas used in the frontend's local physics engine (found in `js/ui.js`) to simulate the thermal behavior of the digital twin. 
(Note: Advanced controller logic like MPC and Carbon Intensity calculations are designed to run on the backend).

---------------------------------------------------------
1. BASELINE ENVIRONMENTAL TEMPERATURE
---------------------------------------------------------
The base temperature starts with the live API weather (in Celsius) and applies a seasonal artificial modifier to simulate extreme conditions when requested via the UI dropdown.

Formulas:
T_base = T_api + Modifier
  - Summer Modifier: +4.5 °C
  - Winter Modifier: -11.0 °C
  - Spring/Fall Modifier: -3.0 °C

---------------------------------------------------------
2. THERMAL GAINS (SOLAR & OCCUPANCY)
---------------------------------------------------------
A. Solar Gain (T_solar)
The sun contributes heat following an inverted V-curve that peaks at 14:00 (2:00 PM), attenuated by live cloud cover.

Formula:
Peak_Proximity = |Current_Time - 14.0|
T_solar_raw = Max(0, 8.0 - (Peak_Proximity * 1.5))
Cloud_Attenuation = 1.0 - (CloudCover_Percentage / 100 * 0.7)
T_solar = T_solar_raw * Cloud_Attenuation

B. Occupancy Gain (T_occ)
Each occupant radiates body heat which mathematically increases the indoor temperature.

Formula: 
T_occ = Occupant_Count * 0.14 °C

---------------------------------------------------------
3. HVAC CAPACITY AND HEAT EXCHANGE
---------------------------------------------------------
The HVAC system attempts to pull the raw environmental temperature down (or up) to the user's target temperature. It has a physical thermodynamic ceiling (max capacity).

A. Raw Deviation
Deviation_raw = (T_base + T_solar + T_occ) - T_target

B. HVAC Thermodynamic Limit (ΔT_hvac)
The HVAC is physically rated to alter the indoor temperature by a maximum of 10.0 °C.
If |Deviation_raw| <= 10.0:
    ΔT_hvac = Deviation_raw   (HVAC succeeds perfectly)
Else:
    ΔT_hvac = Sign(Deviation_raw) * 10.0   (HVAC runs at 100% and fails to reach target)

C. Heat Exchange (kW)
The physical energy exchanged to move the air temperature is calculated at 1.08 kW per degree Celsius of required thermal delta.
Formula:
Heat_Exchange_kW = -(ΔT_hvac * 1.08)

D. HVAC Power Usage (%)
Power percentage is linearly scaled based on how much of the HVAC's maximum capacity (10.0 °C delta) is currently being utilized.
Formula:
HVAC_Power_% = (|ΔT_hvac| / 10.0) * 100

---------------------------------------------------------
4. PREDICTED HEAT LOAD
---------------------------------------------------------
This represents the physical energy (kW) constantly being dumped into the room by the environment that the HVAC must fight against.

Formulas:
Solar_Load_kW = (T_solar / 8.0) * 8.5 kW
Occupancy_Load_kW = Occupant_Count * 0.15 kW
Total_Predicted_Load_kW = Solar_Load_kW + Occupancy_Load_kW

---------------------------------------------------------
5. Q-PREDICTED VECTORS
---------------------------------------------------------
The UI vector percentages visualize the relative impact of each heat source.

Formulas:
- Solar Radiation (%): Min(100, (T_solar / 8.0) * 100)
- Occupancy Gain (%): Min(100, (Occupant_Count / 20.0) * 100)
- Envelope Drift (%): Min(100, |Final_Deviation_from_Target| * 9.0)
- Thermal Decay (%): 5% baseline + algorithmic jitter

---------------------------------------------------------
6. CARBON INTENSITY (BACKEND ARCHITECTURE)
---------------------------------------------------------
While the local frontend calculates instantaneous HVAC Power (%), the actual cumulative Energy Economics (Cost + CO2 footprint) are delegated to the backend controller.

Backend Formula Reference:
Energy_Used_kWh = (HVAC_Power_% / 100) * System_Max_Capacity_kW * Time_Delta
Carbon_Emitted_kg = Energy_Used_kWh * Carbon_Intensity_Grid_Factor (e.g., 0.4 kg CO2/kWh)
Cost = Energy_Used_kWh * Time_of_Use_Rate ($/kWh)
