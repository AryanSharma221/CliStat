This document outlines the exact mathematical formulas used in the
simulation's physics engine to model thermal behavior.

=================================================================
1. BASELINE ENVIRONMENTAL TEMPERATURE
=================================================================
Starts with live Chennai weather (OpenWeatherMap API, metric units)
and applies seasonal modifiers for the UI dropdown.

T_base = T_api + SeasonalModifier
  Summer (day 150-240):  +4.5 °C
  Winter (day <60, >300): -11.0 °C
  Spring/Fall:            -3.0 °C

Fallback (no API): Summer=26°C, Winter=15°C, Default=21°C

=================================================================
2. FIVE-VECTOR Q_PREDICTED HEAT LOAD
=================================================================
Based on architecture §3 Module 5. Each vector is computed in kW
and converted to temperature gain via thermal capacitance.

A. Solar Load (Q_solar)
   Uses getSunIntensity() from sun.js for cloud-attenuated curve:
   
   baseSolar = sin((hour - 6) / 12 × π)      [0..1, peaks at noon]
   cloudFactor = 1 - (cloudCover% / 100) × 0.85
   sunIntensity = baseSolar × cloudFactor
   
   Q_solar = sunIntensity × WindowArea × SHGC × SolarIrradiance
   
   Parameters:
   - WindowArea = 8.0 m² (total glazing)
   - SHGC = 0.4 (Solar Heat Gain Coefficient)
   - SolarIrradiance = 1000 W/m² (standard peak)
   
   Result: 0 to ~1.28 kW at clear-sky noon

B. Occupancy Load (Q_occupancy)
   ASHRAE 55 standard: seated person ≈ 120W metabolic heat
   
   Q_occupancy = OccupantCount × 0.12 kW/person

C. Envelope Load (Q_envelope)
   Heat leaking through building envelope from outdoor temperature:
   
   Q_envelope = U × A × max(0, T_outdoor - T_target) / 1000
   
   Parameters:
   - U = 0.35 W/(m²·K) — insulation factor
   - A = 45 m² — building envelope area
   
D. Thermal Decay (Q_decay)
   Stored heat from previously irradiated furniture re-radiating:
   
   Q_decay = Q_solar × 0.08 (8% re-radiation fraction)

E. Total Predicted Heat Load
   Q_total = Q_solar + Q_occupancy + Q_envelope + Q_decay

   Temperature gain via thermal capacitance (C = 100 kJ/°C):
   ΔT_gain = (Q_total / C_thermal) × 3600  [°C per hour]

=================================================================
3. Q-VECTOR DISPLAY (Dashboard Percentages)
=================================================================
Each vector shows its PROPORTIONAL contribution to total load:

Solar%    = (Q_solar / Q_total) × 100
Occupancy%= (Q_occupancy / Q_total) × 100
Envelope% = (Q_envelope / Q_total) × 100
Decay%    = (Q_decay / Q_total) × 100

These sum to 100%, showing judges the actual breakdown of where
heat is coming from.

=================================================================
4. HVAC CONTROLLER ALGORITHMS
=================================================================

A. Vision Predictive Mode — Feed-Forward PID Controller
   
   error = T_current - T_target
   P = Kp × error                        [Proportional]
   I = Ki × Σ(error × dt)                [Integral, clamped ±50]
   D = Kd × (error - prevError) / dt     [Derivative]
   FF = Kff × Q_predicted                [Feed-Forward]
   
   Output = clamp(P + I + D + FF, 0, 100)  [% power]
   
   Tuning: Kp=1.5, Ki=0.1, Kd=0.05, Kff=0.2
   
   The feed-forward term is the key innovation: it uses the
   predicted heat load to PRE-COMPENSATE before the error grows,
   rather than waiting to react (like a standard PID).

B. Standard Reactive Mode — Bang-Bang Controller
   Simple on/off with hysteresis deadband:
   
   if T_current > T_target + 0.5: power = 100%
   if T_current ≤ T_target - 0.5: power = 0%
   else: maintain previous state
   
   This is the "dumb thermostat" baseline for comparison.

=================================================================
5. HVAC CAPACITY AND HEAT EXCHANGE
=================================================================

A. HVAC Thermodynamic Limit
   Max capacity: 5.0 kW (≈1.5-ton AC unit)
   Max temperature pull: 10.0°C
   
   maxHvacPull = 10.0 × (powerPct / 100)
   
   if |Deviation| ≤ maxHvacPull:
       ΔT_hvac = Deviation        (HVAC succeeds)
   else:
       ΔT_hvac = sign × maxHvacPull  (HVAC at capacity)

B. Heat Exchange (kW)
   exchangeKw = -(ΔT_hvac × maxCapacity / maxDelta)
   exchangeKw = -(ΔT_hvac × 0.5)  [kW per °C]

=================================================================
6. ENERGY & CARBON ECONOMICS
=================================================================

A. Energy Consumption
   E_smart    = Σ (powerPct/100 × maxCapacity × Δt_hours)  [kWh]
   E_baseline = Σ (bangbangPct/100 × maxCapacity × Δt_hours) [kWh]

B. Cost (Time-of-Use Pricing, India)
   Peak rate (4pm-9pm):    ₹8.0/kWh
   Off-peak rate:          ₹4.5/kWh
   
   Cost = E × rate_at_time

C. Carbon Footprint
   Grid intensity: 0.82 kg CO₂/kWh (India average)
   
   CO₂ = E × 0.82  [kg]

D. Savings
   Energy saved = ((E_baseline - E_smart) / E_baseline) × 100  [%]
   Cost saved   = Cost_baseline - Cost_smart  [₹]
   CO₂ avoided  = CO₂_baseline - CO₂_smart  [kg]

7. ADDITIONAL AI MODULES (Available)
=================================================================

- ModelPredictiveController: Horizon optimization (12 steps, 60min)
  evaluates 52 candidate power schedules and picks lowest-cost
- SelfTuningPID: Gradient descent auto-tuning of PID gains
- RLAgent: Q-learning reinforcement agent with state discretization
- KalmanFilter: Sensor noise filtering (process noise q=0.02)
- MultiSensorFusion: Weighted fusion of multiple temperature sensors
- AnomalyDetector: Detects open windows, unexpected heat, HVAC degradation
- SmartBlindController: Predictive blind closing based on solar forecast
- StochasticOccupancyModel: 4-signal fusion (WiFi, noise, appliances, time)
